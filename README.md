# ASTPP: The Open Source VoIP Billing Solution

ASTPP is a robust and widely trusted **Open Source VoIP Billing Solution**, meticulously designed for **FreeSWITCH**. It offers comprehensive prepaid and postpaid billing capabilities, complete with advanced call rating and precise credit control. Beyond core billing, ASTPP provides a rich suite of features to empower VoIP businesses of all sizes.

---

## Key Features

* **Flexible Account Management:** Seamlessly handle both prepaid and postpaid accounts.
* **Multi-level Reseller System:** Empower your resellers with personalized management tools.
* **Calling Card Support:** Integrate calling card services with ease.
* **Least Cost Routing (LCR):** Optimize call routing for cost efficiency.
* **DID Management:** Efficiently manage your Direct Inward Dialing numbers.
* **IP & SIP Termination:** Facilitate diverse termination services.
* **Dynamic Rates Management:** Easily configure and update your call rates.
* **Free Package Management:** Offer flexible free calling packages.
* **Integrated Payment Gateway:** Streamline transactions with built-in PayPal support.
* **Comprehensive Invoicing & Billing:** Generate detailed invoices and manage billing cycles.
* **Insightful Reports:** Access various reports for business analysis and decision-making.
* And many more features tailored for the VoIP industry!
